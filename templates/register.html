Artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming the future of biometric authentication systems. Speaker recognition technology can identify and verify individuals using unique voice characteristics such as pitch, tone, frequency, and speaking patterns. This system improves security by allowing users to authenticate themselves using only their voice. Advanced deep learning models like ECAPA-TDNN can generate highly accurate voice embeddings for each speaker. These embeddings act as unique voice fingerprints and help the system distinguish one person from another. Voice biometrics is now widely used in banking, smart devices, virtual assistants, and secure access systems around the world.
